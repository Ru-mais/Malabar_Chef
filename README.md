# 🍛 Malabar Chef Restaurant

Welcome to the digital home of **Malabar Chef**, Dammam’s premier destination for authentic, high-end Kerala Malabar cuisine. 

Our digital showcase is crafted to mirror the luxury and authenticity of our physical dining experience—blending traditional culinary heritage with cutting-edge, cinematic web design.

---

### ✨ The Digital Experience

This website is not just a menu; it is a visual journey into the art of Malabar cooking. Designed with a custom "Cream Vanilla & Cherry Cola" brand aesthetic, the platform leverages advanced fluid motion graphics to bring our culinary story to life before you even step through our doors.

**Highlights Include:**
- **Cinematic Entrance:** A buttery smooth, high-fidelity splash sequence featuring our signature Beef Curry and Porotta.
- **The Art of Dum Timeline:** Custom-coded SVG motion sequences that visually draw our heritage story as you explore the page.
- **Dynamic 3D Interactions:** Premium menu cards and carousels that react fluidly to your cursor, engineered with GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform) physics.
- **Bespoke Typography:** Featuring luxurious Calligraphy script for chef signatures alongside clean, modern Japanese-inspired sans-serif body text.

### 📍 Our Location
We are proudly located on **Prince Talal Street, Dammam**. Step out of the digital world and into ours to experience the flaky butter porottas and organic spice blends you see beautifully showcased here.
